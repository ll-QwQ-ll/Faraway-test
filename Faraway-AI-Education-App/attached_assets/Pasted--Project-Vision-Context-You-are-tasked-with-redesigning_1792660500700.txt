# Project Vision & Context
You are tasked with redesigning and building an AI-driven education web application named **"Faraway"**. 
We are moving away from dark, heavy tech themes or generic colorful templates. Instead, the design must be **clean, precise, futuristic, mature, and highly interactive**, yet ethereal and AI-driven.

## Core Design & Visual Language
1. **Background:** 
   - Pristine, clean **pure white** (`#FFFFFF`) across the major layout.
   - **Subtle Ambient Effect:** Occasionally, extremely soft, ethereal, and faint pastel rainbow light leaks/aurora gradients drift slowly across the background to give a subtle sense of depth and life, never overwhelming the clean white canvas.
2. **Icons & AI Elements (The Spectrum Engine):**
   - **Rainbow Flowing Strokes:** Icons feature continuous rainbow spectrum gradients that flow and move along the icon contours (like active AI computation), rather than static colors.
   - **Interactive Mouse Responsiveness:** Icons and core UI elements dynamically respond to the user's mouse position (tilt, gradient shift, or slight magnetic attraction).
   - **Translucent 3D Depth:** Subtle glassmorphism, depth layers, and clean shadows give elements a premium, tactile 3D feel without heavy neon glow.
3. **Animations & Interactivity:**
   - **High Interactivity:** Hover, click, and drag states trigger rich visual feedback.
   - **Micro-interactions:** Icons feature fluid transformations, particle aggregation, or active spectrum scanning when AI features are engaged.
   - **Scroll-driven Rhythm:** Content reveals smoothly and responsively as the user scrolls through the page.
